# Hushfeed privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Hushfeed is a browser extension that hides posts on x.com and twitter.com that match words, phrases or accounts chosen by the user.

## What Hushfeed does with your data

- **Your settings** (keywords, accounts, exceptions and preferences) are stored locally in your browser using the extension storage API. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Page content.** To decide whether a post matches your rules, Hushfeed reads the text and author name of posts displayed on x.com and twitter.com. This happens entirely inside your browser. The content is not stored, recorded or transmitted.
- **Statistics.** Hushfeed keeps a local counter of how many posts it has hidden. It stays on your device.

## What Hushfeed does not do

- It does not collect personal information, browsing history or analytics.
- It does not make network requests, use remote code, show ads or sell data.

## Deleting your data

Remove the extension, or use "Reset everything" in Hushfeed's settings, and all stored data is deleted.
