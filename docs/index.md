# Hushfeed privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Hushfeed is a browser extension that hides posts on x.com and twitter.com that match words, phrases or accounts chosen by the user.

## What Hushfeed does with your data

- **Your settings** (keywords, accounts, exceptions and preferences) are stored locally in your browser using the extension storage API. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
- **Page content.** To decide whether a post matches your rules, Hushfeed reads the text and author name of posts displayed on x.com and twitter.com. This happens entirely inside your browser. The content is not stored, recorded or transmitted.
- **Statistics.** Hushfeed keeps a local counter of how many posts it has hidden. It stays on your device.

## What Hushfeed does not do

- It does not automatically collect personal information, browsing history or analytics.
- It does not make network requests, use remote code, show ads or sell data.

## Voluntary feedback by email

The **Feedback & support** buttons open your email app with a draft addressed to zng.itc@gmail.com. The draft contains the Hushfeed version and prompts for your report or suggestion. It does not automatically include your rules, browsing history, page content or screenshots.

You can edit or discard the draft. No email is sent until you send it through your email app. If you do, the recipient receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. Your email provider handles sending the message; Hushfeed has no built-in email submission service.

## Deleting your data

Remove the extension, or use "Reset everything" in Hushfeed's settings, to delete its locally stored settings and statistics. This does not delete emails you have chosen to send through your email app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
