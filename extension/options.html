        About

        Hushfeed works entirely in your browser. Your rules are stored on this device only, and nothing is sent anywhere. It does not collect analytics.

        Keyboard shortcut: Alt + Shift + H turns filtering on or off. You can change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
